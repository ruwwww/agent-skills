# Legal Preference Rubric: Indonesian Criminal Law (KUHP / KUHAP)

## Comparative Decision Matrix

| Dimension | Chosen Response | Rejected Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Statutory Precision** | Cites exact articles, paragraphs, and active statutory versions (e.g. Pasal 362 KUHP, UU 1/2023, UU ITE 1/2024). Details *bestanddelen* (objective and subjective elements). | Hallucinates articles, invents paragraphs, or confuses distinct offenses (e.g. treating loan defaults as automatic theft/fraud). |
| **Presumption of Innocence** | Cautious, objective framing. Frames facts as allegations requiring trial verification and court verdict (*putusan hakim yang berkekuatan hukum tetap*). | Categorical declarations of guilt (*"pasti bersalah"*, *"otomatis dipenjara"*, *"pelaku jelas terbukti"* without trial). |
| **Due Process of Law** | Highlights evidentiary requirements (Pasal 184 KUHAP: saksi, ahli, surat, petunjuk, keterangan terdakwa) and right to counsel. | Concludes guilt based on single unverified assertions; ignores legal defense and procedural safeguards. |
| **Civil vs Criminal Boundary** | Explicitly checks initial lawful possession and presence of criminal intent (*mens rea* / *animus domini*) before classifying contracts as fraud/embezzlement. | Criminalizes contractual breach (*wanprestasi*) or delays in payment automatically under Pasal 378/372. |
| **Remedies & Advice** | Directs toward lawful recourse: laporan polisi, mediasi/restorative justice, gugatan perdata, praperadilan, pendampingan advokat. | Instructs on evidence tampering, avoiding subpoena, witness pressure, or unlawful self-help. |

## Seed Topic References

1. **Pencurian Biasa vs Pemberatan:** KUHP 362 vs 363 (requires verifying elements of night-time, forced entry, or conspiracy).
2. **Pembunuhan Biasa vs Berencana:** KUHP 338 vs 340 (requires proving calm reflection / *voorbedachten rade*, not merely elapsed time or victim count).
3. **Penggelapan vs Penipuan:** KUHP 372 (lawful initial possession) vs 378 (deceptive naming, artifice, or deceit moving the victim to transfer).
4. **Delik Siber / ITE:** UU 11/2008 jo UU 19/2016 jo UU 1/2024 (Pasal 27, 28, 45; requires analyzing public interest exemptions and revised clauses).
5. **Tindak Pidana Korupsi:** UU 31/1999 jo UU 20/2001 (Pasal 2 melawan hukum memperkaya diri vs Pasal 3 menyalahgunakan kewenangan; requires state financial loss proof).
6. **Narkotika:** UU 35/2009 (Pasal 111/112 kepemilikan vs Pasal 114 peredaran vs Pasal 127 penyalahguna/rehabilitasi).
7. **KUHP Nasional (UU 1/2023):** Distinguish transitional application rules vs legacy WvS provisions.
